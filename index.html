We want you to feel confident when you purchase from us. This policy explains when a refund may be requested and how the process works.

Last updated: September 21, 2026

Refund eligibility

Refund requests may be submitted within 5 calendar days of the original purchase date, subject to the terms below.

To request a refund, contact our support team using the email address provided below. Please include the name used for the purchase, the email address associated with the order, and any information needed to identify the transaction.

How refunds are handled

1. Submit your request

Send your refund request to beatrizcostasilva250@gmail.com within the applicable refund period.

2. Request review

We will review the request and may ask for additional information when necessary to locate or verify the transaction.

If the request is approved, the refund will be submitted to the original payment method.

After an approved refund is submitted, the time for the funds to appear in your account can depend on your card issuer, bank, or payment provider.
